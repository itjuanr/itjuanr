### `$ whoami`

Mid-level Systems Analyst at **Deloitte**, working on a large, critical **MES** (Manufacturing Execution System) for the mining industry: the system that tracks and controls production in real time. In that world, a poorly written requirement isn't just a backlog problem, it's an operational risk.

I started in **test analysis**. Two years of hunting failures in that system taught me every flow and business rule, and led to a promotion to own its requirements: elicitation, functional specification, data modeling, UAT and knowledge transfer. That testing background still shapes how I work: I write specs with validation in mind from the start.

I also build software with **TypeScript, React and Node**. My largest project, **Ocean's Store**, is a sales platform with real payments, running in production.
